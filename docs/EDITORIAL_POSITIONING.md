# Editorial positioning — iScience-oriented development

## Central contribution

A reproducible, browser-based multiscale framework that transforms an openly available connectomics dataset into interactive 3D and hand-anchored augmented-reality exploration while keeping biological provenance explicit and testable.

## What would make the work publishable

The visual demonstration alone is insufficient. A strong manuscript should add:

1. quantitative identity/topology/connectivity validation;
2. device performance benchmarks;
3. a principled multiscale/LOD strategy for substantially larger neuronal sets;
4. a biologically meaningful demonstration such as male-specific or sexually dimorphic circuitry;
5. evidence that the framework generalizes beyond a single hard-coded circuit;
6. open code, fixed data manifests and versioned methods;
7. optionally, a controlled spatial/task-learning comparison after technical validation.

## What the paper is not

- not a reproduction of a Cell figure;
- not a claim that AI inferred neuronal connectivity;
- not a voxel-perfect mobile rendering of the 8 nm EM volume;
- not merely an AR classroom activity;
- not evidence of educational benefit without an appropriately designed study.

## Proposed manuscript story

1. Modern connectomics is information-rich but difficult to explore outside specialist platforms.
2. MaleCNS v1.0 provides an authoritative, open test case spanning a complete male Drosophila CNS connectome.
3. An AI-assisted, human-validated software pipeline converts fixed source data into a browser-compatible representation.
4. Topology/connectivity-preserving scientific constraints are explicitly tested.
5. The same data can be explored in conventional 3D and a markerless hand-anchored camera AR mode.
6. Quantitative fidelity and performance determine the useful scale of each representation.
7. Sexually dimorphic/male-specific circuitry provides a biologically meaningful demonstration.
8. Reproducibility is maintained through code, dataset version, query settings, manifests and automated validation.

## Working title

**From connectome to augmented reality: a reproducible multiscale framework for interactive exploration of the Drosophila nervous system**

Graphical-abstract phrase:

**Putting a connectome in your hand**
